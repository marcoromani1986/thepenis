LAIT NOIR was founded in London with a single conviction: fragrance should feel like an intimate memory rather than a performance for others.

We work exclusively with high-grade natural accord bases — pairing rich steamed milk notes with raw cacao, iris root, and charred woods. Each formulation is aged slowly in small batches to preserve its natural luminosity and skin warmth.

Our perfumes are not designed to fill a room. They are designed to be discovered — by someone standing close enough to notice.
